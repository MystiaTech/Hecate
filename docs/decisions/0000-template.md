# ADR 0000: Short decision title

- Status: proposed
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Owners: repository maintainers
- Related issues and pull requests: links

## Context

Describe the public-safe problem, constraints, privacy boundary, and evidence.

## Decision

State the chosen behavior and its permission, data, compatibility, accessibility,
license, and operational consequences.

## Alternatives

Summarize credible alternatives and why they were not selected.

## Validation and rollback

List tests, success measures, monitoring, migration, and a safe rollback path.

## Consequences

Record benefits, costs, residual risks, follow-up work, and any expiry or review
date. To change an accepted decision, create a new ADR that supersedes it.
